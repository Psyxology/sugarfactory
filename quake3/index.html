QUAKE III ARENA

The free 1999 demo: fight bots in the arena. The game downloads about 50 MB the first time, then starts right in your browser.

Move W A S D · Aim mouse · Shoot left click · Jump space · Change weapon mouse wheel
In the menu pick Single Player to fight bots. Press Esc for the menu and to free your mouse.
